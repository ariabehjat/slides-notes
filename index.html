Everything in one place

Built for the way you actually study

No feature toggles hidden behind a subscription. It's all just here.

Highlights write into the PDF

Not a proprietary overlay — real annotations saved straight into the original file. Open it in Preview, Skim, or anywhere else and they're there.

On-device OCR

Bad scan? Recognized automatically on import — no waiting, no upload. Apple's Vision framework, entirely local, German & English.

Rich, page-aware notes

A full A4 note editor per document, plus notes pinned to individual pages that fade in and out as you scroll — with a one-tap way to see them all.

Voice notes & dictation

Record and transcribe on the spot, or dictate straight into a note — no audio kept if you don't want it. Fully on-device transcription.

Browser-style split view

Drag one document onto another to study two side by side — they pair up right in the sidebar and stay linked across launches, like tabs in a split-view browser.

Folders, colour tags, whole-folder import

Organize by course and week with real subfolders and colour tags. Import a whole Finder folder — structure and all — in one go.

Exam-prep export

One Markdown file bundling your highlights, bookmarks and notes — weighted toward what you actually flagged as important. Hand it to any AI to build practice questions.

Dark mode, everywhere

PDF pages invert too — white becomes dark grey, text stays legible, highlight colours are preserved. Not just a dark toolbar around a bright page.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No account. No sign-in. No servers. OCR, transcription, and everything else runs entirely on-device. Your library lives in a plain folder you can see, move, or sync yourself — in Documents, or iCloud Drive if you'd rather.
